About · Perihal

DuitBack is an unofficial personal tracker for Malaysian resident individual (BE/B) returns. Caps follow each year's LHDN schedule (YA2023/24 use approximate historical caps; YA2025+ the current one), incl. medical sub-limits and per-child relief; the tax scale is the YA2025 resident scale. Not affiliated with LHDN; no tax advice — always confirm figures in MyTax before filing.
